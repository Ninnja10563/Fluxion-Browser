The native frame gate passed **21 checks** at the release source. Its evidence
artifact is `10439089678`, `Fluxion-0.70.2-preview.1-frame-evidence`.

- With the sidebar collapsed, native top-edge hover revealed navigation and
  pointer departure over a focused Settings input retracted it to `top=-72`,
  `bottom=0` without moving keyboard focus out of that input.
- Pointer departure into the floating sidebar also retracted navigation.
  Leaving the sidebar then hid it completely and made its surface inert.
- Both sequences passed with the product default and with saved
  `browser.fullscreen.autohide=false`. The latter retained its false user value
  with **zero observed product preference writes**. The fixture restored the
  original preference and provenance in its cleanup.

  began at y=72, but the actual floating surface began at y=6 and ended at
  y=1074 in the 1080px viewport. Its heading began at y=12. The navigation-sized
  reserved gap is therefore absent even while the toolbar is visible.
- Hit tests returned the actual Back, Forward and Reload controls above the
  revealed sidebar. A top-layer address field alone was not accepted as proof
  that the ordinary toolbar controls were accessible.
- Expanded/compact persistence, native Cmd-L/Escape, genuine page-click focus
  transfer, security-panel retention, rounded page clipping and stationary
  pointer tab-close checks remained required and passed.
