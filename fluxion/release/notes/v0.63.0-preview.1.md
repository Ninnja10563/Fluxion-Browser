# Fluxion 0.63 — Group drag-and-drop

Universal macOS preview for Apple Silicon (including M3) and Intel. Gecko is
included; a separate Firefox installation is not required.

- Drag a group heading before or after a tab, split view or another group.
  Insertion feedback marks the whole destination boundary; groups remain intact
  and retain their collapsed state.
- Drop either pane of a split onto a group heading to move the pair together.
  Duplicate selections do not split or duplicate the native container.
- Use Move Group Up/Down to move around ordinary tabs and split views, not just
  other groups. Pinned, stale and cross-workspace targets are rejected.
- Keep Flow's URL tooltips current when a page changes its address without
  reloading. Updates affect only that row and coalesce within a frame.

The native 1,000-tab structure check exercises 30 operations. Seven drag
scenarios verify four real pages retain their document identity, unsaved form
text, JavaScript counter and navigation state with no extra document loads.
Surviving controls, accessible group relationships and native tab order are
checked alongside zero writes to unaffected rows.

Browser Memory remains optional and local; ordinary browsing works without AI.
Group dragging is within one window; Move to Window remains available for
explicit cross-window transfers. Synthetic native-chrome drag tests do not
establish physical M3 input latency or exhaustive trackpad behavior.

Open the DMG and drag Fluxion.app into Applications. This is an ad-hoc-signed
development prerelease, not Apple-notarized or a completed stable product.
